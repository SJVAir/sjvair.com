    What this is, and isn't

    Emissions

    • CARB's facility inventory (CEIDARS): what the district permits, reported or estimated per facility. Cars, farms and other unpermitted sources aren't in it, so a county share is of permitted facilities only.
    • Estimates change. A large year-to-year jump can be a change in how emissions were estimated, not in what the facility emitted.
