What this is, and isn't
  • Sites are the state's lists: CDE's public and private schools and CDSS's licensed child-care centers.
  • Nearness, not exposure. Distances are straight lines between map points; wind, stack height and what each source emits aren't counted.
  • Why these distances. 1,000 ft is where a district must notify parents before permitting a source of hazardous air emissions near a school; ¼ mile is what districts review before siting one; 3,200 ft is CalGEM's health protection zone around wells. More on schools nearby
  • Facilities count where their point is the site, not oil & gas or refinery permit groupings, whose addresses are offices. The {{ pollutant.label }} column sums what the facilities within ¼ mile reported in {{ year }}.
