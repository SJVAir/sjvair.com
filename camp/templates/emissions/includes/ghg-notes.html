What this is, and isn't{% endif %}
  • A climate measure, not a local health one. Greenhouse gases warm the climate; they aren't what people nearby breathe.
  • Only large emitters report (about 10,000 t CO2e a year and up), so most facilities have none.
  • Oil and gas production reports for a whole basin, not one site. Fuel sold by suppliers isn't counted here, and dairies don't report to either program.
