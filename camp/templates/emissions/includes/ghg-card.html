What this is, and isn't

Greenhouse gases warm the climate; they aren't a local health measure. Only large emitters (about 10,000 t CO2e a year and up) report, so most facilities have none. Oil and gas production reports for a whole basin, not one site. Fuel sold by suppliers isn't counted here. Dairies don't report to either program.
