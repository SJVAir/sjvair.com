How to read this page

Confirmed application records come from Pesticide Use Reporting (PUR), which lags roughly a year behind real time. The newest year loaded here is the newest DPR has released, not the most recent year that's happened.

A notice of intent is a plan to spray, not proof that it happened. Growers file it 24–48 hours before a scheduled application and then have up to four days afterward to actually begin, so a notice can show up here before, during, or briefly after the window it names.

Darker shades on the map and bars mean more pounds applied that year, ranked against everything else shown — read the legend for the actual range, since the same shade can mean different amounts on different pages.

A badge like Prop 65, IARC, or CARB TAC means a chemical appears on that list because of what's known about it in general, not how much of it reached anyone here. It says nothing about the amount applied nearby.
