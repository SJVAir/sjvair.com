About this data

Confirmed applications come from DPR's Pesticide Use Reporting program, which releases each year's records one to two years after the fact{% if latest_year %} (the newest full year here is {{ latest_year }}){% endif %}. Planned applications are SprayDays notices of intent, which are plans, not confirmations. Badges mark chemicals on official lists (Prop 65, IARC, CARB); they say a chemical is listed, not how much of it reached anyone.

Read more about the data, the badges, and the caveats. Developers can pull the records through the pesticides API or the Python client.
