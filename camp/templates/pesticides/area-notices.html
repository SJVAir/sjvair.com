What this is, and isn't
  • A notice of intent is a plan, not a confirmed application. A grower files one before applying a California-restricted material; the application may happen up to four days after the scheduled date, or not at all.
  • Only restricted materials need one, so most of the pesticide use on the Overview never appears here. More on notices
  • A notice gives the square-mile section, not the field.
