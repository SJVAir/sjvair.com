{% extends explorer_base %} {% comment %} The skeleton an explorer's area data tabs share, so they read alike however different their data: under the header and tab row, a stat row; then the filters (`tab-filters`) and any other boxes (`tab-aside`) in a column beside the map, with the active-filter chips (`active_filters` [{label, clear_url}]) above it; the table the page's full width under them; then the charts, the tab's related sections, and its notes. A tab fills the blocks it has; with nothing beside the map the side column hides itself (area-pages.sass) and the map takes the row. `tab-body` wraps the lot, for a tab with nothing to show to replace in one go.
